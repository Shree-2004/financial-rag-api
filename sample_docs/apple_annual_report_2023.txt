APPLE INC. - ANNUAL FINANCIAL REPORT 2023

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Apple Inc. reported record revenues of $394.3 billion for fiscal year 2023, a slight decrease of 2.8% from $397.5 billion in 2022. Net income was $97.0 billion, representing a net profit margin of 24.6%. The company generated $114.0 billion in operating cash flow, demonstrating exceptional cash generation capabilities.

INCOME STATEMENT HIGHLIGHTS
- Total Net Sales: $394.3 billion
- Products Revenue: $298.1 billion (iPhones: $200.6B, Mac: $29.4B, iPad: $28.3B, Wearables: $39.8B)
- Services Revenue: $85.2 billion (all-time record, up 9% YoY)
- Gross Margin: $169.1 billion (42.9% gross margin rate)
- Operating Income: $114.3 billion
- Net Income: $97.0 billion
- Earnings Per Share (Diluted): $6.16

BALANCE SHEET HIGHLIGHTS
- Total Assets: $352.6 billion
- Total Liabilities: $290.4 billion
- Total Shareholders Equity: $62.1 billion
- Cash and Cash Equivalents: $29.9 billion
- Total Debt: $109.3 billion
- Debt-to-Equity Ratio: 1.76 (manageable given strong cash flows)

CASH FLOW ANALYSIS
- Operating Cash Flow: $114.0 billion
- Capital Expenditures: $11.1 billion
- Free Cash Flow: $99.6 billion (extremely strong)
- Share Repurchases: $77.6 billion
- Dividends Paid: $15.0 billion

RISK FACTORS
1. China Market Dependency: Approximately 19% of revenues come from Greater China, creating geopolitical risk.
2. Smartphone Market Saturation: iPhone upgrade cycles are lengthening as the market matures.
3. Regulatory Pressure: Increasing antitrust scrutiny in the EU and US regarding App Store policies.
4. Supply Chain Concentration: Heavy reliance on Taiwanese manufacturers (TSMC) for chip production.
5. Currency Headwinds: Significant portion of revenue earned in foreign currencies subject to exchange rate fluctuations.

SEGMENT PERFORMANCE
Americas: $162.1 billion (41% of total revenue)
Europe: $94.3 billion (24% of total revenue)
Greater China: $72.6 billion (18% of total revenue)
Japan: $24.3 billion (6% of total revenue)
Rest of Asia Pacific: $41.0 billion (10% of total revenue)

DEBT ANALYSIS
Apple's total long-term debt stands at $95.3 billion. Despite the high absolute debt level, the company's debt-to-EBITDA ratio is approximately 0.8x, indicating a very low leverage risk. The company's AAA credit rating reflects its exceptional financial strength. Interest coverage ratio is 29.5x, well above the safe threshold of 3x.

LIQUIDITY POSITION
Apple maintains a current ratio of 0.99 and a quick ratio of 0.97. While these ratios are slightly below 1.0, this is not concerning given Apple's consistent and predictable cash flow generation. The company has $30 billion in cash plus $150 billion in marketable securities available.

MANAGEMENT OUTLOOK
Management expects Services segment to continue growing in double digits. iPhone 15 cycle shows strong initial demand. Vision Pro spatial computing product represents a new revenue frontier expected to generate $2-4 billion in fiscal 2024.

AUDITOR'S REPORT
Ernst & Young LLP issued an unqualified (clean) audit opinion. No material weaknesses in internal controls were identified.
