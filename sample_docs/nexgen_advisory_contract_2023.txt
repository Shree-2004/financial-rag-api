RECITALS
WHEREAS, Client desires to engage Advisor for comprehensive financial advisory, portfolio management, and risk analysis services; and
WHEREAS, Advisor represents that it has the expertise and capability to provide such services;
NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — SCOPE OF SERVICES
1.1 Portfolio Management: Advisor shall manage Client's investment portfolio with an initial AUM of $250,000,000 (Two Hundred Fifty Million USD).
1.2 Risk Assessment: Monthly Value-at-Risk (VaR) reports at 95% and 99% confidence intervals.
1.3 Financial Reporting: Quarterly performance attribution reports benchmarked against S&P 500.
1.4 Compliance: Ensure all investments comply with Client's Investment Policy Statement (IPS).
1.5 Advisory Calls: Minimum bi-weekly calls with Client's CFO and investment committee.

ARTICLE 2 — FEES AND COMPENSATION
2.1 Management Fee: 0.75% per annum on Assets Under Management, billed quarterly in arrears.
    - Year 1 Fee Estimate: $1,875,000 (based on $250M AUM)
2.2 Performance Fee: 10% of returns exceeding the hurdle rate of 8% per annum (high-water mark applies).
2.3 Transaction Fees: No additional transaction fees for standard equity and fixed income trades.
2.4 Fee Schedule:
    - Q1 2023 Fee: $468,750 (due April 15, 2023)
    - Q2 2023 Fee: Adjusted based on quarter-end AUM
    - Q3 2023 Fee: Adjusted based on quarter-end AUM
    - Q4 2023 Fee: Adjusted based on quarter-end AUM

ARTICLE 3 — FINANCIAL RISK DISCLOSURES
3.1 Market Risk: Investment returns are subject to market volatility. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
3.2 Liquidity Risk: Certain portfolio positions may have limited liquidity, potentially requiring extended periods to liquidate at fair value.
3.3 Concentration Risk: Client's IPS permits maximum 15% allocation to any single security.
3.4 Credit Risk: Fixed income holdings rated below BBB- (investment grade) are capped at 10% of total portfolio.
3.5 Currency Risk: International equity exposure capped at 30% of portfolio; currency hedging strategy to be agreed quarterly.
3.6 Leverage Policy: No leverage permitted under this agreement without explicit written consent from Client's Board.

ARTICLE 4 — PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKS
4.1 Primary Benchmark: S&P 500 Total Return Index
4.2 Secondary Benchmark: 60/40 Blend (60% S&P 500 / 40% Bloomberg Barclays Aggregate Bond Index)
4.3 Target Return: 10-12% annualized total return over full market cycle
4.4 Maximum Drawdown Tolerance: -15% in any rolling 12-month period before mandatory review

ARTICLE 5 — REPORTING OBLIGATIONS
5.1 Advisor shall provide:
    - Daily: Portfolio NAV and cash position summary
    - Weekly: Market commentary and portfolio snapshot
    - Monthly: Full performance attribution and risk report
    - Quarterly: Comprehensive investment review with forward outlook
    - Annually: Full year audited performance report

ARTICLE 6 — TERMINATION
6.1 Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice.
6.2 Early termination by Client before 18 months shall incur a breakage fee of $500,000.
6.3 Immediate termination permitted in case of material breach, fraud, or insolvency.

ARTICLE 7 — GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

ARTICLE 8 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration under FINRA rules in New York City.
